### 🔬 研究方向

#### 1. 无人机与低空交通物流
* **无人机路径规划**：复杂环境下的无人机航线生成与避障路径规划模型。
* **卡车-无人机联合运输**：空地协同末端配送、协同调度与路线优化模型。
* **低空物流系统优化**：多无人机多任务分配与协同运行模型。

#### 2. 港口与航运物流系统
* **泊位与岸电协同调度**：绿色港口资源配置与作业协同优化模型。
* **内河航运网络规划**：考虑船舶能源/燃料选择的航道网络路径规划与多目标优化。
* **多式联运系统优化**：枢纽港多式联运协调机制与调度模型。

#### 3. 应急物流与韧性交通
* **应急物资调度**：突发事件下的多主体应急响应与物资配送模型。
* **交通网络中断与韧性恢复**：基础设施受损与中断情境下的网络评估与动态调度策略。

### 🔬 Research Focus

#### 1. UAV & Low-Altitude Logistics
* **UAV Path Planning**: Trajectory generation and obstacle-avoidance routing models in complex urban/airspace environments.
* **Truck-Drone Joint Transportation**: Air-ground collaborative last-mile delivery, joint scheduling, and routing optimization models.
* **Low-Altitude Logistics Optimization**: Multi-UAV task assignment and collaborative operational models.

#### 2. Port & Shipping Logistics
* **Berth & Shore Power Collaborative Scheduling**: Green port resource allocation and operational synergy optimization models.
* **Inland Shipping Network Planning**: Waterway route planning and multi-objective optimization considering alternative vessel fuel choices.
* **Multimodal Logistics Optimization**: Hub port multimodal coordination mechanisms and dispatching models.

#### 3. Emergency Logistics & Resilient Transportation
* **Emergency Relief Dispatching**: Multi-stakeholder emergency response and relief delivery models under disruptions.
* **Network Disruptions & Resilience Recovery**: Network resilience assessment and dynamic dispatching strategies under infrastructure damage.
